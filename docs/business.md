# Who it is for and how it earns

[Back to README](../README.md)

## Who uses it

- **Busy professionals and students** who live on WhatsApp and email and lose hours to small tasks.
- **Small shop owners** who chase suppliers and customers all day.
- **Anyone more comfortable speaking their own language** than typing English.

## How we reach them

1. **Start on our campus.** Students are fast adopters, and we can sit with them and fix things daily.
2. **Then working professionals in Hyderabad.**
3. **WhatsApp is the hook.** When Jarvis sends a message for you, the person on the other side sees what it can do. See a task done, invite a friend.
4. **Short demo videos in Hindi and Telugu,** showing real tasks getting done by voice.

## How it makes money

| Plan | Who | Price |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Free** | Everyone, for everyday tasks | Rs 0 |
| **Plus** | Heavy users who want more tasks, more connected apps and the cloud computer | Rs 199 to 499 a month |
| **Team** | Small businesses that want follow-ups, reminders and customer messages handled | Per seat |

Later, UPI payments with approval add a small transaction-based revenue stream.

## Competition

| | What they do | Where Jarvis is different |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT, the Gemini app | Great at answering | Jarvis completes multi-step tasks across your real apps |
| Siri, Alexa | Single voice commands | Jarvis handles work like "find a place, book it and tell my friend" |
| Zapier and automation tools | Powerful, but need setup and technical skill | Jarvis needs nothing but your voice |

And across all of them: Jarvis is built voice-first for Indian languages, and nothing is sent or booked without your approval.

## Markets

- **Primary:** India.
- **Secondary:** WhatsApp-first markets with large Indian-language-speaking populations, starting with the UAE, then Southeast Asia and Bangladesh.
