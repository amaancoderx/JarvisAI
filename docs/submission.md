## One-line summary

Jarvis is a voice-first AI agent that lets anyone just say what they need, in their own language, and gets it done across email, calendar, WhatsApp and the web, asking before it acts.

## Problem

Small digital chores eat everyone's day, and every one means opening another app and typing. Most tools are built for English and keyboards. AI assistants mostly talk instead of finishing the job. And when AI does act, people don't trust it. There is nothing an ordinary person in India can just talk to, in their own language, and trust to get real work done.

## Solution

You talk normally in your language, and Jarvis breaks the request into steps and works through them, inside Gmail, Google Calendar, WhatsApp and any website. It has its own computer in the cloud, remembers people and past chats, and always asks first. A working version is already live.
