The problem

Hundreds of millions of people talk. Software still makes them type.

01

Built for English and keyboards

Most of India is far more comfortable speaking Hindi, Telugu or Tamil than typing English into an app.

02

Assistants talk, they don't finish

Ask a chatbot to book a table and it tells you how. You still open the app and do it yourself.

03

Nobody trusts AI to act

One wrong email to your boss or one wrong booking, and people never use it again.

How it works

From a sentence to a finished task in four steps.

You speak

Gemini Live keeps a real-time conversation going, in your language.

It understands

Gemma routes the request: a quick question, or a task that needs a skill.

It plans and acts

Gemini on Vertex AI plans the steps, calls the right tools and checks each result.

You approve

Anything that sends, books or shares waits for your tap, or your voice: "haan, bhej do".

Trust by design

Safety lives in code, not in a prompt.

The model can suggest. Fixed rules decide. That's why people can let Jarvis act for them.

Approval bound to the exact actionSigned to one recipient and one exact message. Change one word and Jarvis asks again.
Only you can give ordersText inside emails, websites or files can never trigger an action or choose a recipient.
Nothing happens twiceEvery action carries an idempotency key, so a retry never sends a second message.
A switch for everything, and a full audit logPause all sends in one tap. See what Jarvis did, when and why.
